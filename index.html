About Promotezz

Building high-performance operating system utilities, developer documentation, and modular web applications.

Promotezz is an independent software collective founded to create fast, privacy-respecting, and decluttered digital experiences. Operating under the central domain promotezz.cc, we design and maintain specialized tools tailored for performance and developer productivity.

From eliminating telemetry and OS bloatware on Windows systems with StarDebloater, to providing comprehensive technical knowledge via Promotezz Wiki, and curating optimized Modrinth modpacks through Packed, our focus remains on lightweight engineering and user control.
